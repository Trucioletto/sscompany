Hard technology, simple software.

Spinne Software is an Italian company that builds its own technology and its own products. The work is deliberately hard in the places nobody sees, so that what reaches the person using it is simple. Sparkle, a desktop video editor, is the first product.

What the company values

  • Innovation New technology when it makes something genuinely better, not when it is merely new.
  • Craftsmanship The parts nobody sees are built to the standard of the parts everybody sees.
  • Simplicity The difficulty is ours to absorb. What reaches the person using it should be obvious.
  • Ambition Built to stand beside the best software in its category, or not built at all.
  • Transparency What we say the software does is what it does, including where the answer is no.
  • Independence We own the parts that decide what the product can become, and name the ones we rent.

Weave the web

The company is named after the German word for spider. A web is not decoration: it is the structure everything else stands on, and it is built one thread at a time.

A desktop video editor for people who cut for a living, and the first place those values had to survive contact with a real product. What makes it unusual is not the models — it is that they sit inside the editor rather than beside it. An edit the assistant makes is an edit you could have made by hand, and one undo takes it back.

Most of its AI runs on the machine the footage is already on, and the generative work that needs the cloud is metered where you can see it. In private preview.

And then the next one

A product of this kind leaves a great deal behind it that is not the product: accounts and permissions, the services that run the models, the systems that keep the money honest. None of it is interesting, and all of it is what the next product no longer has to build.

Spiders do not spin for the pleasure of it. The structure is what makes everything after it possible.

Sparkle came first because a company is only believable about its second product once it has finished the first. What that took already exists; the next one starts from there.
